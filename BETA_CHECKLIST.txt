MonitorMage - beta test steps
First beta release - package 0.111.2; application version 0.111.0

Try the sections that match how you normally use your computer. You do not
need a second monitor or an HDR display to help test.

1. TRY A LOOK
   Open MonitorMage and choose Gentle contrast from the top dropdown.
   Turn master power off and on to compare. Open another effect and check
   that its own switch and settings work. Watch for flashing sliders or
   black/copied areas below the drawn monitor.

2. TRY BLACK AND WHITE
   Choose Red filter B&W. Open Channel mixer and change Red, Green and Blue.
   The picture should stay black and white, but colored objects should become
   lighter or darker. White should stay white. Try Reset, then save your look
   with Save as. Choose another look, then return to your saved one.

3. TRY A GAME
   Choose Grayscale, then open a game. After the game changes resolution,
   wait about three seconds. Is the game still black and white?
   Press Alt+Tab to return to the desktop, then return to the game.
   If colors do not return, try Bypass look and then turn bypass off again.
   If the game offers Fullscreen, Borderless and Windowed, compare the modes
   you use. Record the exact option name; some fullscreen modes ignore these
   color effects even when MonitorMage is working on the desktop.

4. CHECK THE WINDOW SIZE
   At your usual resolution and Windows text/app scaling, open Settings and
   Application profiles. Check that text fits and buttons remain reachable.
   If you normally switch resolutions or move windows between monitors, try
   that too. Small desktops should get scrollbars. Use Tab to reach a control
   and check that it scrolls into view. Returning to a larger desktop should
   bring back the normal window size.

5. CHECK PROFILES AND STARTUP
   Follow Read me.txt to give a program its own saved look. Switch to and
   away from that program: your previous look should return when you leave.
   If you use automatic startup, enable Start with Windows and Start minimized.
   At your next normal sign-in, MonitorMage should run in the tray without opening
   its editor. Before opening it, check that your saved look and application
   profiles already work. Left-click the tray icon: the editor should show
   the current look. Also try exiting directly from the tray without opening it.

6. CHECK THAT COLORS ARE RESTORED
   With a visible look active, choose Exit from the tray. Your previous colors
   should return. Open MonitorMage again and check that the saved look returns.
   If you normally use sleep, try sleeping and waking the PC with a look active.
   Check that master power still pauses and resumes the adjustments afterward.

7. OTHER DISPLAYS, IF YOU HAVE THEM
   Try your normal screen arrangement. If you already use both SDR and HDR,
   compare both. If you duplicate the picture on two screens, check them both.
   Saturation and Channel mixer should affect all displays; other effects use
   the selected display. Night light has its own display choices.
   If you use Extend mode, enable Night light on both screens. Both should get
   warmer. Exclude one screen: that screen should return to its previous colors.
   Exit from the tray and check that both screens return to their previous colors.
   If you switch HDR on and off, repeat the switch with a look active. Ordinary
   effects should continue; HDR gamma should follow its Also apply in SDR option.

IF YOU FIND A PROBLEM

Please describe:
- What you clicked or changed, step by step.
- What you expected and what happened instead.
- The MonitorMage version, Windows version, graphics card and display.
- Your screen resolution and Windows scaling percentage, if known.
- The game/program name and its Fullscreen/Borderless/Windowed option.
- Whether bypass or master power fixes it; include a screenshot if helpful.

For Saturation or Channel mixer problems, Settings > Color effect report
saves a report on your computer. Nothing is sent automatically. Read it before
sharing: it includes display details and running program names.
